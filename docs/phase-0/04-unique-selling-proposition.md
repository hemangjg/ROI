## Three Pillars

### Pillar 1: Unified Attribution

**What:** Every LLM call attributed to team, user, project, feature, and customer — across every provider.

**Why it matters:** Finance cannot do chargeback without attribution. Engineering cannot optimize what they cannot measure. Attribution is the foundation everything else builds on.

**How we're different:** Provider dashboards show total spend. Cloud FinOps tools attribute cloud resources. We attribute AI tokens natively, with metadata tags designed for LLM workflows.

---

### Pillar 2: FinOps-Native Workflows

**What:** Budgets, alerts, forecasting, chargeback, and executive reporting — built for finance teams, not just engineers.

**Why it matters:** The buyer is often FinOps or finance, not the developer who integrated the SDK. The product must serve both personas with equal quality.

**How we're different:** Dev observability tools (Helicone, LangSmith) show engineers their traces. We show FinOps managers their P&L impact. Different product, different buyer, different retention.

---

### Pillar 3: Governance-First

**What:** Approved model lists, usage policies, audit trails, sensitive data detection, and compliance reporting.

**Why it matters:** CISOs and compliance teams are increasingly involved in AI purchasing decisions. A cost tool without governance will lose enterprise deals to one that has both.

**How we're different:** No existing tool combines cost visibility with governance in an AI-native way. Cloud security tools don't understand token economics. AI observability tools don't do compliance.

## Why Not Build In-House?

| In-house approach | Problem | Our advantage |
|-------------------|---------|---------------|
| Spreadsheet + provider CSVs | Manual, error-prone, not real-time | Automated ingestion |
| Custom logging in each app | Inconsistent schema, no cross-app view | Standardized SDK |
| Internal pricing calculator | Pricing changes weekly across 10+ providers | Maintained pricing catalog |
| DIY governance scripts | No audit trail, no policy engine | Built-in compliance |
| Cloud FinOps tool AI module | Not AI-native, weak token attribution | Purpose-built |

**Build vs. buy threshold:** If AI spend is < $50K/year, build in-house is reasonable. Above $100K/year, the maintenance burden of a custom solution exceeds our subscription cost.
